# 03 - Entanglement and Multi-Qubit Systems

**Level:** Beginner to Intermediate

## Overview
This folder introduces multi-qubit quantum systems, tensor products, quantum correlations, and entanglement through hands-on Qiskit implementations.

The experiments demonstrate how quantum systems can exhibit correlations that cannot be explained using classical independent states.

## Topics Covered
- Tensor products of quantum states
- Two-qubit and three-qubit systems
- Controlled-NOT and controlled-Z gates
- Bell state preparation
- GHZ state preparation
- Quantum correlations
- Entangled versus separable states
- Multi-qubit measurement

## Learning Outcomes
- Construct mathematical representations of multi-qubit states.
- Prepare Bell states and GHZ states using Qiskit.
- Analyze measurement correlations in entangled states.
- Visualize multi-qubit quantum circuits.

## Jupyter Notebook Experiments
Planned experiments include Bell state generation, GHZ circuits, multi-qubit measurements, and entanglement demonstrations.
