# 10 - Quantum Machine Learning

**Level:** Advanced

## Overview
This folder explores Quantum Machine Learning (QML) through Qiskit implementations of quantum feature maps, quantum kernels, variational quantum classifiers, and quantum neural networks.

The experiments investigate how quantum circuits can be integrated with classical machine learning workflows for supervised learning and other data-driven tasks.

## Topics Covered
- Quantum data encoding
- Quantum feature maps
- Quantum kernels
- Quantum Support Vector Machines (QSVM)
- Variational Quantum Classifiers (VQC)
- Quantum Neural Networks (QNN)
- Hybrid quantum-classical models
- Model training and evaluation

## Learning Outcomes
- Encode classical datasets into quantum circuits.
- Construct quantum kernel-based learning models.
- Implement variational quantum classifiers.
- Evaluate models using accuracy, confusion matrices, and related metrics.
- Compare quantum models with classical baselines.

## Jupyter Notebook Experiments
Notebooks will contain beginner-friendly datasets, classification experiments, model training, visualizations, and reproducible evaluations.
