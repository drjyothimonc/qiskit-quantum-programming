# 04 - Quantum Measurement and Simulation

**Level:** Beginner to Intermediate

## Overview
This folder focuses on quantum measurement, measurement statistics, and quantum circuit simulation using Qiskit and Qiskit Aer.

The experiments compare theoretical quantum probabilities with simulated measurement outcomes and explore different methods of examining quantum states.

## Topics Covered
- Computational basis measurement
- Measurement probabilities
- Statevector simulation
- Qiskit AerSimulator
- Measurement shots and counts
- Measurement histograms
- Expectation values
- Simulation of quantum circuits

## Learning Outcomes
- Execute quantum circuits on local simulators.
- Interpret measurement counts and probability distributions.
- Compare theoretical and simulated results.
- Visualize quantum measurement outcomes.

## Jupyter Notebook Experiments
Notebooks will demonstrate ideal statevectors, sampling-based measurements, histograms, and simulation workflows.
