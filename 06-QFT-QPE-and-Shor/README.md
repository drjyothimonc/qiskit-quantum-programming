# 06 - Quantum Fourier Transform, Phase Estimation, and Shor's Algorithm

**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced

## Overview
This folder explores three important concepts in quantum algorithm design: Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT), Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE), and Shor's factoring algorithm.

The experiments focus on mathematical derivations, quantum circuit construction, phase estimation, and demonstrations of quantum period finding.

## Topics Covered
- Discrete Fourier Transform and Quantum Fourier Transform
- QFT circuit implementation
- Inverse Quantum Fourier Transform
- Controlled unitary operations
- Quantum Phase Estimation
- Eigenvalues and quantum phases
- Quantum period finding
- Shor's factoring algorithm

## Learning Outcomes
- Implement QFT and inverse QFT circuits.
- Construct and simulate Quantum Phase Estimation circuits.
- Interpret measurement outcomes as estimated eigenphases.
- Understand the mathematical foundation of Shor's algorithm.

## Jupyter Notebook Experiments
Notebooks will include QFT examples, phase estimation demonstrations, and small-scale period-finding and factoring exercises.
