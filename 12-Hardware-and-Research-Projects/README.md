# 12 - Quantum Hardware and Research Projects

**Level:** Advanced and Research

## Overview
This folder focuses on quantum hardware execution, circuit transpilation, benchmarking, and reproducible research using Qiskit.

The experiments connect theoretical quantum computing concepts with practical simulation, hardware-aware circuit design, and experimental evaluation.

## Topics Covered
- Quantum hardware architectures
- IBM Quantum execution workflows
- Quantum circuit transpilation
- Hardware connectivity and native gates
- Circuit depth and gate count analysis
- Simulator versus hardware comparisons
- Quantum algorithm benchmarking
- Quantum Machine Learning research experiments
- Reproducible experimental design

## Learning Outcomes
- Prepare circuits for hardware-compatible execution.
- Analyze circuit complexity and transpilation results.
- Execute experiments on simulators and supported quantum hardware.
- Record and interpret benchmarking results.
- Design reproducible research experiments.

## Jupyter Notebook Experiments
Research notebooks will document experimental objectives, methods, circuit designs, software dependencies, results, limitations, and interpretations.

Hardware-based experiments may require IBM Quantum account access and suitable backend availability.
