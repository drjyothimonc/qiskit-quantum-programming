# 01 - Quantum Fundamentals

**Level:** Beginner

## Overview
This folder introduces the fundamental principles of quantum computing through mathematical explanations, Python programming, and hands-on Qiskit experiments.

Learners will explore qubits, quantum states, superposition, probability amplitudes, and measurement.

## Topics Covered
- Classical bits versus quantum bits
- Computational basis states
- Quantum superposition
- Statevector representation
- Quantum probability amplitudes
- Measurement and the Born rule
- Bloch sphere representation

## Learning Outcomes
By completing these experiments, learners will be able to:
- Represent single-qubit quantum states mathematically.
- Prepare and manipulate quantum states using Qiskit.
- Calculate and interpret measurement probabilities.
- Visualize quantum states and circuits.

## Jupyter Notebook Experiments
Notebooks will be added progressively, with explanations, code, circuit diagrams, results, and exercises.
