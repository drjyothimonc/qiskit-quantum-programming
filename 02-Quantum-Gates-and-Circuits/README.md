# 02 - Quantum Gates and Circuits

**Level:** Beginner

## Overview
This folder explores quantum logic gates, unitary transformations, and quantum circuit construction using Qiskit.

The experiments demonstrate how quantum gates transform qubit states and how multiple gates can be combined to perform quantum computations.

## Topics Covered
- Identity and Pauli X, Y, Z gates
- Hadamard and phase gates
- S, T, RX, RY, and RZ gates
- Controlled quantum gates
- Unitary matrices and state transformations
- Quantum circuit construction
- Gate composition and circuit visualization

## Learning Outcomes
- Understand the mathematical operations performed by quantum gates.
- Implement single-qubit and controlled gates using Qiskit.
- Construct and visualize quantum circuits.
- Analyze the evolution of quantum states through circuits.

This section will contain gate demonstrations, matrix calculations, statevector simulations, and circuit visualization exercises.
